How to read your Task List — "Your Day Tomorrow"

Report reading guide · CNG training program · v2026-08-06 · English edition

1 · What it is and who it is for

This is your list of what is due TOMORROW, wired into the process: it arrives every night at 8:00 PM, Monday–Friday (cron active since Aug 6), one per PM, covering your projects. Every line talks ONLY about REAL tasks that exist in your schedule — nothing invented. The header carries the golden rule: a job only starts if everything before it is ready.

Purpose: to let you plan tomorrow BEFORE tomorrow: what the schedule says is due, what you were asked and is still open, and what you are waiting on from others — plus the inspection radar so you never walk into a city appointment unprepared.

2 · Anatomy — what every element means

Section / labelWhat it means
YOUR GENERAL PENDING ITEMSAt the top: requests to you that don't belong to one specific project, each with "pending for X days".
§1 · YOUR DAY TOMORROW (per project)What is due per your schedule — each task with its label and one plain line saying what to do.
WORK · IN PROGRESSA trade is executing: your job is to document — progress photos in CompanyCam WITH a description; never let anything get covered up before you check it and photograph it.
YOUR TASKSomething YOU do (PM inspection, CC report, WO, SOW…): "do it on site with photos, not from your phone".
PASSIVE PROCESSCuring, drying…: it runs on its own, no sub needed — just protect the conditions (e.g., keep everyone off the concrete).
CITY INSPECTIONA city inspection: EVERYTHING the city will check, checked by you and photographed BEFORE calling — if something fails you lose the appointment and days of work.
MATERIAL ON THE WAYInformational ETA: confirm with Romina that the delivery is real; never book the sub without the material on site.
🏛 INSPECTIONS ON THE RADAR (5/3/1)The bot warns you 5, 3 and 1 days before each inspection ("X DAYS LEFT" chip) and offers to review the building codes together before you call. If you already reviewed it with the bot, it stays quiet.
§2 · WHAT YOU WERE ASKED, STILL OPENRequests from the meetings and from Jose: 🔴 = urgent; each with "pending for X days". They don't vanish — they age in plain sight.
§3 · WHAT YOU ARE WAITING ON FROM OTHERSYour open waits (Romina, subs, management) — so you chase them before they block you.

3 · Where it comes from

  • §1 comes from your JobTread schedule (refreshed every 20 min): only tasks that EXIST in the trade's schedule.
  • §2/§3 come from the meeting records and Jose's directives (one more reason to say everything at the 2:30 meeting).
  • Inspection reminders follow the 5/3/1 cadence and switch off once you review the inspection with the bot.
  • Payments never appear here — that lane belongs to Milagros/Jonas.

4 · What to do when you receive it

1. That same night, build your tomorrow from §1: route, which site first, which photos and checklists are due.
2. If tomorrow has a CITY INSPECTION or the radar says "1 DAY LEFT": message the bot TONIGHT and review the codes — you can still make it; a failed appointment costs days of work.
3. In §2, close the 🔴 and the oldest items first ("pending for 20 days" is a debt everyone can see).
4. In §3, chase what you're waiting on first thing in the morning — don't let your day die waiting.
5. If anything in §1 doesn't match reality on site, say it at the 2:30 meeting so it gets re-dated: the schedule is a mirror of reality.

5 · Common reading mistakes

"PASSIVE PROCESS = call the sub." No: it runs on its own (curing, drying). Your only action is protecting the conditions.
"The ETA is a firm date." It's informational (an estimate): confirm with Romina — and never book the sub without material on site.
"Old pending items expire." They don't: they roll over every night with their day counter, and they are the same items management sees in your Your Next Week.
"I'll prep the inspection the same day." Too late: that's why the radar starts 5 days out. A lost appointment costs days (real case: red tags for wrong sequence and plans not on site).
"Where are my payments?" Not here: sub payments and income run through Milagros/Jonas.
