How to read your PM Weekly Evaluation

Report reading guide · CNG training program · v2026-08-06 · English edition

1 · What it is and who it is for

This is your evaluation of the week that just closed. It arrives every Saturday 8:00 AM (automatic) — one PDF for you, one copy for Jose. The week closes Friday at 11:00 PM: everything you see is a frozen snapshot of that moment. You are evaluated Friday vs Friday: what counts is what you CLOSED between last Friday's close and this one.

Purpose of this report: to show you, project by project, your real schedule against the plan that was locked last Friday, your documentation, and your weekly numbers using the honest algorithm (v20). Only WORK appears (Action ▸ + Critical Path ★ + city inspections ◆) — payments never show in the gantts.

The honest algorithm (v20) — how your % is computed

  • ON TIME = finished WITHIN the week. The main % counts ONLY these.
  • PAST DUE ››› = started but extended into next week. It subtracts: you never see 100% if anything was extended.
  • NOT FINISHED = had a date inside the week and was still open at close.
  • OVERDUE = tasks whose date already passed, still open at Friday's close. This is NEUTRAL data: work to close or schedule to reprogram — not a verdict.

3 · Where the numbers come from

  • Everything comes from JobTread frozen at Friday 11 PM (the close), compared against the previous Friday's 11 PM baseline (the plan).
  • Documentation ✓/✗ comes from what is registered in JobTread for each trade's schedule.
  • AI adoption comes from your messages with the assistant (DM + group) on working days.
  • The official report's numbers rule — nobody recalculates them on the side.

4 · What to do when you receive it

1. Look first at the orange ››› and the not-finished tasks: that is what you carry into the new week. Bring it squared to the 2:30 meeting (closing = check + real dates squared).
2. Go through the OVERDUE list: it is your cleanup list — each item either gets closed or gets reprogrammed in JobTread. Do not let it pile up.
3. Every ✗ in documentation is a concrete to-do: upload the SOW/WO/takeoff or complete the checklist in CompanyCam. Remember: the After checklist unlocks the sub's payment.
4. Compare against last week's Your Next Week: that plan is exactly what you were evaluated against. This week's one will be your next evaluation.
5. If a number looks wrong, ask the bot BEFORE Monday — do not recalculate on your own.

5 · Common reading mistakes

"My % is the project's progress." No: the weekly % only counts what you finished ON TIME. Project progress is the other column (ACCUMULATED PROGRESS).
"Orange means I'm being blamed." No: reschedules are presented neutrally — they are the schedule reflecting reality. But they do subtract from the week's full compliance.
"Overdue is a punishment." It is neutral: part of what is open is pending plan cleanup, not work left undone. But it must be cleaned up.
"Where are the payments?" Not here: money lives in the paid/collected summary of the Project Department Weekly Evaluation and in its COLLECTIONS page.
"I was graded for not moving the schedule on Tuesday." Never: evaluation is Friday vs Friday, never day-by-day punctuality.
