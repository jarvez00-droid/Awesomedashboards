COO Track · Session 2 — Reading the 4 Management Reports

Track COO · Rey Direction module · 30–45 min Sources: canon §9.4–9.5 · REPORTERIA_MASTER §A
A report the COO reads is worth exactly the decisions it produces: which site he visits, which chain he unblocks, which PM he corrects. The official report's numbers RULE — the COO never recalculates them on the side.

1 · Objective

This section exists so you know what "done" looks like. Read the official management reporting in ≤20 min/day and turn each report into ONE kind of decision.

The reporting laws (canon §9.5) — the COO obeys them like everyone

  • Law 0: baseline = Friday 11:00 PM. In the PM reports: GRAY = the Friday photo (the ideal), GREEN = what really happened.
  • Law 1: evaluation = what got CLOSED Friday vs Friday — never "the schedule didn't move" (schedules may be in correction).
  • Law 2: the official report's numbers RULE — discrepancies are raised, never overwritten.
  • Law 3: "what was said rules over what was scheduled."
  • Law 4: after 3 PM, JobTread is the truth of the day.
  • Style law C2: reschedules are presented in neutral, without blame — they are the schedule reflecting reality.
  • The honest algorithm (PM Weekly Evaluation): % = only tasks finished ON TIME; extended = orange + ››› arrows. The number is already honest: don't soften it, don't harshen it.

3 · The real case

The week of 29-jun (case #2): the company produced HALF of projection because EXECUTEs were dated with their chain at 0% — in Martha, ELECTRICAL ROUGH IN was dated 7–8 jul with SELECT VENDOR & SEND WO and CONFIRM SCHEDULE WITH SUB at 0%. The day arrived and there was no electrician. A COO reading the Next Week report against the golden rule catches this BEFORE the week starts — that is what "reading to decide" means.

4 · The exercise

With the latest real Daily Report + Project Dept Weekly: extract (1) three decisions — one visit, one unblock, one PM correction; (2) one item that is NOT yours and route it (money → Administrator, client → CEO); (3) one number you were tempted to recalculate — and didn't (say why the official number rules).

5 · What gets evaluated

  • Given a real report set: the 3 decisions in under 15 minutes.
  • Never quotes a number different from the official report's.
  • Never phrases a reschedule as blame (law C2); evaluates people only Friday vs Friday.
