COO Track · Session 5 — Measuring the Team: Outputs, Friday vs Friday, Recognize

Track COO · Rey Direction module · 30–45 min Sources: GUIA_CEO 2026-07-03 · law C2
Measure OUTPUTS observable in the systems — inspection passed, payment out, photos with description, task closed — never hours or sensations. And measure to RECOGNIZE, not to hunt culprits: metrics without recognition are surveillance; recognition without metrics is noise. Together, they are direction.

1 · Objective

This section exists so you know what "done" looks like. Read the team the GUIA_CEO way: outputs, honest context, Friday-vs-Friday, one verification question before any conclusion — and same-day, specific recognition.

2 · Content

The 3 rules (GUIA_CEO §1)

  • Outputs observable in the systems — the systems already record everything alone; look at them with fixed criteria, daily.
  • The systems already exist — dashboard, bitácoras, reports, ledger. Nothing new to invent.
  • Measure to RECOGNIZE — the goal is that each person ends the day knowing "cumplí, me puedo ir tranquilo".

COO lens vs CEO lens

  • The CEO runs the closing ritual and the semáforo; the COO reads the same fichas for the OPERATIONAL side: which PM's field execution needs his presence, which "red" is a system lie (stale data, broken sync).
  • His own semáforo row: payments not overdue/handed off · COO entries in bitácoras · ≥1 signal answered per day.

The advisor's warnings the COO most needs (GUIA_CEO §6)

  • Activity ≠ result — before scolding a "red", ask what they CLOSED this week.
  • The one who chats most is not the one who produces most — anchor to outputs, never message volume.
  • Data punishes those who work without registering — and sometimes the system lies. A new red deserves ONE question first: real, or stale data?
  • The example comes from the top — in the July audit the worst numbers were the owners'. The team adopts the system when they see the COO closing his own checklist.

Friday vs Friday is law (C2)

  • Evaluation = what got CLOSED between Friday baselines. Reschedules in neutral. "Vencidas" = a cleanup list, not a verdict. Never blame by day.

Recognition mechanics (GUIA_CEO §4)

  • Same day, specific, with the data. Model line: "Rey — vi tus fotos de [obra] y la nota de calidad en la bitácora. Así se ve el rol de COO. Cumplido."
  • When not met — question, not reproach: ¿qué te bloqueó? + ¿qué necesitas? + ¿para cuándo? If the blocker was the system's (or yours — an undelivered decision), say so.

3 · The real case

The Jeff paradox (GUIA_CEO §6.1): red across the dashboard, 15 days silent — and still he invoiced the month's biggest milestone ($82,780 DRYBOX) and his framing forecast was more honest than the schedule's. A COO who reads only the semáforo punishes his best closer; a COO who first asks "what did you CLOSE this week?" directs. The semáforo opens the conversation; it never ends it.

4 · The exercise

With the latest Project Dept Weekly + one PM's weekly evaluation: produce (1) one specific recognition message, with the real data; (2) one question-not-reproach for an unmet check (blocker + need + date); (3) one "red" you verified as real-vs-stale BEFORE concluding — and what the verification was.

5 · What gets evaluated

  • His weekly reads produce recognition + questions — never day-by-day blame.
  • Zero conclusions on a new red without the one verification question.
  • His own Día Cumplido sustained: certification = N consecutive days with the 4 checks ✅ [PROPUESTA N=10 hábiles — VERIFICAR CON JOSE].
