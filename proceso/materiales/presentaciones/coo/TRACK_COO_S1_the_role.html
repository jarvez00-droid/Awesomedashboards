COO Track · Session 1 — The COO Seat: Plan = Reality

CNG Training Program Track COO · Rey Direction module · 30–45 min
THE KEY (Jose, 2026-08-04): MANAGE THE WORK THE WAY IT WAS PLANNED IN THE SCHEDULE. The margin (~40%) is earned managing the schedule, not executing the work. The COO is the human guarantee that the plan and reality are the same thing — on every site, every day.

1 · Objective

This section exists so you know what "done" looks like for this session. By the end you can explain, in 2 minutes and without notes: what the COO owns, what he never does, and why this seat exists inside THE KEY of CNG.

2 · Content

What the COO OWNS (roles 2026-07-03)

  • Schedule vs reality — visits sites; what the schedule says active must be really happening, right sub, right pace. Confirms or corrects at the 2:30 PM daily meeting (official; = 3:30 PM Peru). After 3 PM, JobTread is the truth of the day.
  • Critical path — nothing leaves its critical path; threats get unblocked on-site or escalated immediately.
  • Quality, all jobs — done right and passes inspection the first time.
  • On-site resolution — he shows up when the PM can't solve it alone.
  • The trail rule — every visit leaves photos in CompanyCam (with description) or a note dictated to the bot. A visit without a trail did not happen for the system.

What the COO does NOT do

  • The money — the Administrator is the gatekeeper: "nothing is paid without passing through her". He requests and updates.
  • The client / commercial strategy — that is the CEO's; the client always hears first from the PM.
  • The PM's job — he directs, verifies and unblocks; the PM executes SOW, WOs, checklists, photos. A COO doing PM work stops directing the operation.

The 5 behaviors (the root cause)

Rigor · attention to detail · foresee · solve · insatiable proactivity. The mental rule: never accept-and-react — prevent it, or search for solutions insatiably until the schedule is met no matter what. The COO models this for everyone: the example comes from the top.

"JobTread es un espejo de lo que está pasando en la realidad" (Jose, P3) — the PMs feed the mirror, the Backoffice records it, the COO verifies it against the ground, one level above the Lead PM of the designed org.

3 · The real case

The role's own starting point (GUIA_CEO, audit 2026-07-03): at that date — ZERO COO mentions in the 11 project bitácoras, a payment 21 days overdue (Rhodes Surveyor) in his name, ~54 pings unanswered in 18 days. The field instinct was right; the trail was missing. The role exists precisely to turn that instinct into verifiable direction — and the fastest way for the team to adopt the system is to see the COO closing his own checklist first.

4 · The exercise

On 2 real active projects: say out loud — (1) where is its critical path TODAY, (2) when was your last visit WITH a trail, (3) what will you check tomorrow on site to confirm the schedule is telling the truth. Then dictate one real site note to the bot so the trail mechanic is exercised.

5 · What gets evaluated

  • States mission + the 4 ownerships + the 3 boundaries, without notes.
  • His last 5 site visits all have a CompanyCam/bot trail.
  • Día Cumplido (working version, [VERIFICAR CON JOSE]): ≥1 visit with trail · schedule-vs-reality confirmed/corrected at 2:30 · zero his-name payments overdue without action/hand-off · ≥1 signal answered.
