  3. TRADE: CREATE TAKEOFF REPORT (2. Purchases, Romina) → TRADE: CONFIRM TAKEOFF DETAILS (1. PM Tasks, el PM) → TRADE: SECURE PRICING & ETA (2. Purchases, Romina) → TRADE: PURCHASE MATERIALS (5.
