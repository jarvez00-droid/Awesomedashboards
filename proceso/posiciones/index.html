The rule that binds all 8: every CNG position serves THE KEY — manage the job the way it was planned in the schedule — and is evaluated under the same laws: Friday-vs-Friday, the official report's numbers rule, and the 5 behaviors (rigor · attention to detail · anticipate · solve · relentless proactivity).

1Un sub terminó su trabajo. ¿Por cuántas manos pasa su pago antes de salir?A sub finished the work. How many hands does the payment pass through before it goes out?
Tres. (1) El PM aprueba EN SITIO con el checklist After lleno; (2) el Backoffice revisa el checklist y el expediente (segundo candado); (3) la Administradora aplica sus 4 filtros y ejecuta. Nadie paga solo.Three. (1) The PM approves ON SITE with the After checklist filled; (2) Backoffice reviews the checklist and the file (second lock); (3) the Administrator applies her 4 filters and executes. Nobody pays alone.
2¿Planning puede llamar al cliente para aclarar una medida?Can Planning call the client to clarify a measurement?
No, nunca. Planning NUNCA habla directo con el cliente — todo pasa por el vendedor (ASM). Un solo canal = cero versiones cruzadas de lo que el cliente pidió.No, never. Planning NEVER talks directly to the client — everything goes through the salesperson (ASM). One channel = zero crossed versions of what the client asked for.
3¿Quién es el único par de ojos de la empresa DENTRO de la obra todos los días?Who is the company's only pair of eyes INSIDE the jobsite every day?
El PM. Compras, Backoffice, la Administradora y Planning están en Perú; el CEO dirige; el COO visita pero no vive en cada obra. Por eso el PM confirma el takeoff EN OBRA, documenta con fotos y es el primero en avisar al cliente.The PM. Purchases, Backoffice, the Administrator and Planning are in Peru; the CEO directs; the COO visits but doesn't live on every site. That's why the PM confirms the takeoff ON SITE, documents with photos, and is the first to tell the client.
